## 2026-10-28

### Added

- Initial playable Level 1 of Deficientezinho: a wheelchair platformer with
  rolling, hopping (short tap or taller hold), a toggleable boost, braking,
  and numpad support, so everything works without pressing two keys at
  once.
- A settings screen (`O`) for boost mode (toggle/hold), language (Português
  (Portugal), the default, or English) and character (the logo-drawn
  Deficientezinho, the default, or a classic wheelchair avatar), remembered
  in the browser.
- Checkpoints, a battery/health system fed by pickups, cart enemies that can
  be stomped, and a star rating (finish, 80% of tokens, par time).

### Changed

- Dropped the placeholder "EFFICIENZINO" branding (title-screen credit line,
  finish-line banner, the opening sign, the browser tab, README) in favor
  of the game's own name; the finish line now reads "FINISH".
- Added a tagline under the title, "um passeio do cara***", shown on the
  title screen, the browser tab and in the README.

### Fixed

- Coins that were buried in the ground near the curbs and the first park
  hill.
